Systems

Data and setup

Put it on your phone. Open the page from your home server, then use Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the menu → Install app / Add to home screen in Chrome on Android.

Wall display. The Wall display button opens the household display: energy, home, travel, a screensaver and a night clock, made for a wall tablet or the TV. Each mode has its own link, such as display.html#home, so each screen can open its favourite.

All prices include VAT. Gas in cubic metres is converted to kWh with a volume correction of 1.02264 and a calorific value of 39.5 MJ/m³, so gas figures are close estimates. Smart meter readings arrive a day behind, so charts cover full days up to yesterday.

Simulations, projections and savings are estimates to help you decide what to look into. They aren't financial advice or quotes.
